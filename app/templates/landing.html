How it works

1

Upload your file

Drop in a .docx, .xlsx, .pdf, .jpg, .jpeg or .png file from any browser, including your phone.

2

Check the preview

Sesh converts it to PDF in the cloud. The preview is the exact PDF that will be printed.

3

Send it to the office printer

Choose copies, paper size and colour. The office computer picks the job up and hands it to Windows.

  • Word .docx
  • Excel .xlsx
  • PDF .pdf
  • Images .jpg .jpeg .png

Your files stay private, and the status stays honest

Private storage

Documents are stored privately and shown only to signed-in people who are allowed to see them. There are no public links.

Administrators and workers

Administrators add workers, see all print activity and manage the office computer. Workers see their own documents and jobs.

Status you can rely on

“Sent to printer” means Windows accepted the job, not that paper came out. If the outcome is uncertain, Sesh flags it for an administrator instead of printing again.

Before you start

  • You need a Windows 10 or 11 computer at the office, connected to the printer, switched on and signed in. Sesh Agent runs on it and only makes outbound connections.
  • The printer must already be installed in Windows. Sesh uses the existing driver and never installs one.
  • Word and Excel files are converted by Sesh, not by Microsoft Office, so page breaks and layout can differ. Always check the preview.
  • Old .doc and .xls files, and password-protected files, are not supported.
