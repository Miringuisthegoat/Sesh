Before you print

Word, Excel and image files are converted to PDF by Sesh, not by Microsoft Office, so layout can differ from what you see in Word or Excel. Always check the preview before printing.

  • PDFs are print-ready and uploaded as-is. Nothing is converted, so what you see in the preview is what prints.
  • Word: page breaks may move; text boxes, shapes, charts and columns are not reproduced.
  • Excel: formulas show their last saved result; charts and images are skipped; very wide sheets are shrunk to fit the page width.
  • Old .doc/.xls files and password-protected files (including PDFs) are not supported.
