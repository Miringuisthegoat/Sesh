Before you print

Conversion is done by Sesh, not by Microsoft Office, so layout can differ from what you see in Word or Excel. Always check the preview before printing.

  • Word: page breaks may move; text boxes, shapes, charts and columns are not reproduced.
  • Excel: formulas show their last saved result; charts and images are skipped; very wide sheets are shrunk to fit the page width.
  • Old .doc/.xls files and password-protected files are not supported.
